CounselGrid Inc. - Frequently Asked Questions

ABOUT US

Who we are
We are CounselGrid Inc., a small US team that fixes one problem well:
slow follow-up on new client enquiries.

Who we help
Solo and small law firms across the United States, 2 to 10 attorneys.
Firms that are great at the law, but lose new clients to slow follow-up.

The problem we see
An enquiry arrives at night, or while your office is busy. Nobody calls
back in time. The person signs with the next firm, and a quiet enquiry
becomes a matter worth thousands you never billed.

How we fix it
We build you a new enquiry form that checks itself, plus a one page
follow-up board that shows every open enquiry, its stage and the fee at
risk. So a live lead never sits unseen.

We work with firms in all 50 states. We are based in Austin, Texas, and
we do everything remotely.


20 QUESTIONS WE GET ASKED

1. Who do you work with?
Solo and small US law firms, 2 to 10 attorneys. If you have a website
form, an answering service, or both, we can help without changing how
you work.

2. What exactly do I get each month?
Two things. A new enquiry form that checks itself, and a one page
follow-up board showing every open enquiry, its stage and the fee at
risk. You also get the setup, the handover and support while you are
with us.

3. How much does it cost?
Our fee is $1,200 a month, billed monthly. Your first month is $2,400.

4. Why is the first month $2,400?
It covers the build. We design your form, connect it to your board,
load your practice areas and go live with you. After that it is $1,200
a month.

5. Is there a contract or a minimum term?
No. It is month to month. You can cancel any time.

6. Are there any other fees?
No hidden fees from us. The only extras are tools you already pay for,
like Airtable or your phone provider.

7. How long does setup take?
Your form is live in about 48 hours. The board and the full handover
are done within 5 business days.

8. How do I get started?
Call (512) 555-0148 or email hello@counselgrid.com. We book a 30 minute
call, look at how enquiries reach you today, and send a simple one page
plan.

9. What happens after I pay?
You get a kickoff call within one business day. We build the form and
the board, show you both, make one round of changes, and go live with
you. Your team also gets a short training call.

10. What does "checks itself" mean?
Every enquiry is checked before it is saved. We look for a name, a
phone or email, a practice area, and a phone with 10 digits. Bad
entries are stopped before they reach your board.

11. What happens to a bad enquiry?
It never lands in your main list. It goes to a separate errors list
with a note saying exactly which field to fix, and the person is told
the same thing on screen. Nothing is lost.

12. Where do good enquiries go?
Straight onto your follow-up board as an open row, with the date, the
practice area, how they reached you, and the fee at risk.

13. What is the follow-up board?
One page, always current. It shows every open enquiry, its stage
(new, contacted, booked, signed, closed) and the fee at risk, so anyone
can see what needs a call today.

14. What is "fee at risk"?
A dollar figure for what the matter is likely worth. We set it with you
by practice area, so a quiet enquiry reads as a number, not just a
missed call.

15. Do you chase the enquirer for me?
No. The board tells you who to call and how long they have waited. Your
team makes the call. That keeps it personal and keeps you in control.

16. Do I have to change my website, phone or software?
No. We give you the form to link from your site or send to your
answering service. Your phone, email and case software stay as they are.

17. Does it work with Clio, MyCase or the tools I already use?
The board runs on Airtable and works alongside your tools. We can add
links back to Clio or MyCase. If you want deeper integration, tell us on
the call and we will say honestly what is possible.

18. Who on my team can see the board?
As many people as you like. We set the access with you, so partners,
assistants and your answering service only see what they should.

19. What if I want to cancel?
Email us any time before your next billing date. We stop billing at the
end of that month, hand over your data, and there is no cancellation
fee.

20. Is my client data safe?
Yes. Data is stored in the US, access is limited to your team and ours,
and we sign a confidentiality agreement. We never sell or share your
data.
